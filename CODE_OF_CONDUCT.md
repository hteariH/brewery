# Code of Conduct

Brewery follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, use GitHub's "Report content" option on the comment, issue or discussion, or contact the maintainer, [@hteariH](https://github.com/hteariH), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block people who break these rules.
